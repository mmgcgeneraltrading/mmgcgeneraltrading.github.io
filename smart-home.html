OPTIONAL SECURITY ADD-ON

Watchdogs Rapid Response

Watchdogs is positioned as the physical rapid-response partner, while MMGC handles the technology. A verified alarm or camera incident can be escalated to Watchdogs for dispatch within contracted coverage.

Check Rapid-Response Coverage
1 · Alarm / camera detects an event
2 · Customer / authorised monitoring verifies the situation
3 · Watchdogs receives the security escalation
4 · Response team dispatches where covered
5 · Police / medical / fire escalation when necessary

Rapid response is an optional separate service and depends on the final MMGC–Watchdogs agreement, service-zone coverage, operating hours and customer contract. The planned model uses local response stations with approximately 0–30 km service zones; availability must be confirmed before sale.

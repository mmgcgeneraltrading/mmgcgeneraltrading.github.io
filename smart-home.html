Before choosing your equipment

Choose a reliable connection

Plan Starlink availability, line-of-sight, Wi-Fi coverage, subscription arrangements and an optional backup connection.

Start with useful automations

Common use cases include outdoor lights at sunset, motion-triggered notifications, away-mode routines and energy scheduling.

Design for safety and resilience

Prioritise alarm connectivity, equipment surge protection, UPS or backup power and secure user accounts.

Common questions

Does Starlink automatically give security cameras satellite connectivity?

No. Cameras usually connect to a local router over Wi-Fi or Ethernet; that router may use Starlink as its internet connection.

Will devices keep working if the internet is off?

Some local functions remain available, but remote notifications and cloud services may stop. Behaviour depends on the installed products and network design.

Can I begin with one room?

Yes. A phased setup can begin with the most useful cameras, network coverage or smart lights before adding other rooms.

Watchdogs Rapid Response

Watchdogs is positioned as the physical rapid-response partner, while MMGC handles the technology. A verified alarm or camera incident can be escalated to Watchdogs for dispatch within contracted coverage.

Check Rapid-Response Coverage
1 · Alarm / camera detects an event
2 · Customer / authorised monitoring verifies the situation
3 · Watchdogs receives the security escalation
4 · Response team dispatches where covered
5 · Police / medical / fire escalation when necessary

Rapid response is an optional separate service and depends on the final MMGC–Watchdogs agreement, service-zone coverage, operating hours and customer contract. The planned model uses local response stations with approximately 0–30 km service zones; availability must be confirmed before sale.

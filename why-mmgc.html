WHAT WE DO NOT PROMISE

Professional support without unrealistic guarantees.

MMGC can help organise, cost, source, format and review a tender response, but no bidder should rely on a consultant or supplier to guarantee an award. Evaluation decisions remain with the procuring entity.

For BOQ-based tenders, the client/bidder remains responsible for independently checking final quantities, arithmetic, VAT treatment and the tendered amount before submission.

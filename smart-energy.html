Before choosing your equipment

Understand the load first

List critical loads such as the router, CCTV recorder, alarms and essential lighting before choosing an inverter or batteries.

Monitor use and performance

Compatible equipment can show battery charge, PV output and consumption. Remote access depends on network availability.

Use competent installers

Solar and electrical installations need correctly sized components and safe, compliant installation.

Common questions

Can my security cameras stay powered during load shedding?

Potentially, if a properly sized and installed backup power system supports the camera, router, recorder and any required switches.

Does every inverter have an app?

No. Remote monitoring depends on the inverter model, monitoring gateway and compatible service.

Can MMGC combine energy and security enquiries?

Yes, you can request a coordinated scope covering priority loads, network equipment and alarm backup requirements.

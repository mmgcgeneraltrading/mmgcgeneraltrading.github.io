Before choosing your equipment

Choose the right connectivity

Wi-Fi cameras need reliable network coverage and often an electrical supply. Battery/solar and LTE models work differently.

Protect your recordings

Compare local NVR/microSD recording, optional cloud storage, passwords, account sharing and retention policies.

Plan your response

An alarm notification is not the same as security dispatch. Any Watchdogs or drone response depends on a separate authorised arrangement.

Common questions

Can wireless cameras work without Wi-Fi?

Only camera models with another connectivity method, such as LTE, can transmit remotely without Wi-Fi. Some cameras still record locally while offline. Exact behaviour depends on model.

Do wireless cameras need power cables?

Many Wi-Fi cameras do need external power. Battery/solar models can be wire-free, but placement, sunlight and recording requirements must be considered.

Can MMGC help combine alarms and CCTV?

You can request a site assessment for a compatible system covering entry sensors, cameras, notifications, storage and optional response discussions.

SIMPLE PROCESS

From information to finished documents

Choose service

Select CV, cover letter, combined package or application support.

Provide details

Sign in, upload a PDF if available and check extracted information.

Review draft

MMGC prepares the requested documents; confirm corrections and final fee.

Collect or receive

Download, receive or collect approved documents; follow status in your account.

Prepare these details

  • Your full name and working telephone number.
  • Education, qualification and training records, where available.
  • Employment history and dates of employment.
  • Skills and contactable referees, if authorised.
  • Existing CV as a PDF, if you have one.
  • Vacancy notice, employer name and deadline for a tailored letter.

MMGC can help clients who do not have an existing CV; the intake form is still useful.

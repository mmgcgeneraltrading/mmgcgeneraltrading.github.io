STARLINK & VEHICLES

Internet and tracking are two different things.

Where Starlink hardware and an eligible service plan permit vehicle/mobile use, MMGC can quote connectivity installation. GPS/GNSS tracking still requires a dedicated tracker or telematics device.

Vehicle internetEligible Starlink/mobile connectivity or another suitable network source.
TrackingDedicated GPS/GNSS tracker and compatible platform.
VideoFront, rear, cabin and driver-monitoring camera options.
PowerAppropriate vehicle power and backup design for installed devices.

Specific Starlink hardware, plans and in-motion permissions change by market and plan. MMGC should confirm eligibility before selling a vehicle-connectivity package.

SuperPay is a proposed unified payment gateway connecting participating online shops and platforms to Lesotho's M-Pesa and EcoCash wallets through authorized financial partners.

Apply as a merchantTry payment demo
Prototype / not accepting live payments. SuperPay is in planning and demonstration. MMGC is not representing this as an approved payment provider. No connection to M-Pesa, EcoCash, Visa or Mastercard has been activated; merchant applications are expressions of interest only.
How the gateway works

From checkout to verified payment

1

Merchant connects

A shop or platform applies and, when approved for a future service, connects its checkout to the SuperPay API.

2

Buyer chooses wallet

The buyer selects M-Pesa or EcoCash and approves a provider-issued payment request on their phone.

3

Secure confirmation

A licensed payment partner verifies the result, handles settlement and SuperPay notifies the merchant through a signed webhook.
